# Day-of rehearsal — run the door before the night

**Two hours. Eight people. Everyone on their own phone.**

We are rehearsing **Dandiya Night** on the practice site. Nothing you do here
touches the real event, the real money, or the real ticket-holders.

Your job is not to make it work. Your job is to **find the places where it
doesn't**, while it is still cheap to fix.

- **Site:** [test.dcica.org](https://test.dcica.org) — the practice copy
- **Real site (do not use today):** events.dcica.org
- **You will get a ticket by email before the day.** Keep it. Print it if asked.

---

## The one rule

> **Only use your own ticket, or one handed to you for the rehearsal.**

The practice site contains a copy of real ticket-buyers' email addresses. If you
go poking at someone else's order, a real person can receive a confusing email
and never tell us. So:

- Buy only with **your own** email address.
- Do not open the **Email** screen in admin.
- Stay on the Dandiya event. Do not touch Garba, Navratri or Diwali.

Everything else — scan it, break it, tap it twice — is fair game.

---

## Before you arrive

**Log in before you leave home.** The practice site limits sign-in attempts per
network, and eight people on the hall WiFi all count as *one*. If everybody logs
in at the door, some of you will be locked out for fifteen minutes.

1. Go to **test.dcica.org/test-login**
2. Enter **your username** from the table below and the shared password
   (in the briefing email)
3. You should land on your own screen. Leave the tab open.

If you get locked out anyway: turn WiFi off, use mobile data, try again.

## What "pass" looks like

The door screen answers in **one colour, one word, one instruction**. You are
checking that the answer is *unmistakable at arm's length, in hall lighting*.

| Colour | Sound | Means |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | two rising notes | Let them in |
| 🟠 **Amber** | one flat note | **Stop and ask.** Something needs a human |
| 🔴 **Red** | two falling notes | Do not let them in |

**Amber is not a pass.** If anyone treats amber as "fine, go through", say so
out loud — that is exactly the confusion we are here to find.

**Tap "Start scanning" at least once**, even if you plan to type codes. On
iPhones the sound does not work until you do.

## Round 1 — Buying a ticket (15 min)

*Everyone already has a ticket by email. This round tests buying a **new** one.*

**Three people only** (Coordinator picks who). The rest watch.

1. On your phone, go to **test.dcica.org**
2. Find **Dandiya Night** and tap **Buy tickets**
3. Buy **1 entry**, using your own name and email
4. At the card screen use the practice card:

   | | |
   |---|---|
   | Card number | `4242 4242 4242 4242` |
   | Expiry | any future date |
   | CVC | any 3 digits |
   | ZIP | any |

5. **One of the three** uses `4000 0000 0000 0002` instead — this card is
   *supposed* to be declined.

**Pass:** you land on a confirmation page with a QR code, and an email arrives
within a minute or two carrying the same code.
**The declined one:** you should be able to try again without losing your order.

> ⏱ If the email takes more than ~3 minutes, tell the Coordinator. Do not buy again.

## Round 2 — The door (20 min)

Door A and Door B both open `/scan` and tap **Start scanning**.

### 2a. A normal arrival

Guests queue up and show the QR from their email.

**Pass:** 🟢 **ADMITTED**, rising sound, and it tells the volunteer
**"Give wristband"**.

### 2b. The same person again — *the important one*

Scan the **same** guest a second time.

**Pass:** 🟠 **ALREADY IN**, with **the time they first came in**, flat sound,
and **"No wristband — ask before letting them through"**.

**Fail:** anything green, or a screen that looks the same as the first scan.

### 2c. The answer has to stay put

With a verdict still on screen, **wave a different guest's QR at the camera.**

**Pass:** the screen does **not** change, makes **no** sound, and adds a note:
*"Another code was scanned and ignored — finish this guest first."*

Now tap **Next guest** — the button **inside the coloured banner** — and scan
again. It should work normally.

> 🪤 **Deliberate trap.** Further down the screen there is a different button,
> **"Done — next guest"**. Tap that one instead and the banner stays up and the
> camera stops responding. **Tell us if this catches you out** — it is the
> single most likely mistake on the night.

### 2d. A family — and nobody has a name

Scan **Manish Popli's** ticket (family of 4), then **Naresh Garg's** (party of 10).

You will see 4 or 10 rows **with no names on them**. That is correct: Dandiya
sells by headcount and never asks who is coming. **The real night will look
exactly like this.**

1. Admit **three** people individually
2. Scan the same code again

**Pass:** the button now reads **"Admit remaining 7"**, not "Admit all 10".
Admit them all. The verdict should read **Admitted 7 · of 10** and tell you how
many wristbands to hand over.

### 2e. Finding someone with no code

Someone left their phone in the car.

Type **the first two or three letters of their surname** into the door's search
box — you should not have to type the whole thing.

**Pass:** they appear, you can tap them, and you can admit them without a QR.

## Round 3 — Paper (20 min)

The Guest wrangler hands out the printed sheets.

1. Scan a **single-ticket** printout. Should behave exactly like a phone.
2. Scan the **multi-ticket** printout — several QRs on one page. Watch whether
   the scanner grabs the wrong one. Try it at arm's length, then closer.
3. **Glare test:** hold the paper under a light at an angle and try again.
4. When the camera refuses, **read out the code printed under the QR** and have
   the door type it.

Then, on a phone, open the **ticket link** (`/t/...`) for someone already
admitted.

**Pass:** it says **"Already used"** with the time.

> 📄 A printed sheet can never show "already used". Paper always looks valid.
> That is a fact about paper, not a bug — but it is why the door screen, not the
> ticket, is the source of truth.

## Round 4 — Walk-ups and cash (20 min)

### 4a. Door B (has a cash box)

A guest arrives with no ticket and wants **three entries and two sets of sticks**.

1. Tap **"No ticket — walk-up sale"**
2. Tap the entry item, then use the **− 3 +** stepper to reach 3
3. Add 2 sticks
4. Check the button reads **"Take cash $XX & admit 3"** — the *admit* number
   must be **3**, not 5
5. Take the cash, tap it

**Pass:** one order, three tickets, the headcount goes up by **3**, and the form
stays open ready for the next customer.

> 💵 **There is no "amount tendered" box and no change calculator.** You work out
> change yourself. That is how it is built today — not a fault to report.

### 4b. Door A (no cash box) — the check that matters

Try to do the same thing.

**Pass:** there is **no walk-up sale button at all**, and an unpaid guest shows a
plain line of text — *"Owes $X — a till holder has to take this."* — with **no
button to tap**.

**Fail:** you can see a cash button, even a greyed-out one.

### 4c. Door price vs online price

Entry online is **$12**. At the door it should be **$15**.
Sticks are **$5 both ways**.

**Pass:** the walk-up total uses the door prices, and sticks are not marked up.

## Round 6 — Volunteers (15 min)

Volunteer desk (`volcoord`) opens **`/volunteer/checkin`**.

Four practice volunteers exist, with codes like `VOL-DANDIYA-2026-0001`
(on your printout).

1. Type a code in full — **the whole thing, including `VOL-`**. This screen does

2. Tap **Sign in**
3. Tap the same code again — try to sign in twice. Nothing bad should happen.
4. Tap **Sign out**
5. Try to **sign out someone who never signed in** → expect a refusal
6. Open **`/volunteers`**

**Pass:** the roster shows who is **On site**, and hours appear after sign-out.

## Round 7 — Breaking it on purpose (20 min)

This is the most valuable twenty minutes of the day.

### 7a. Wrong event

The Guest wrangler has a **Navratri** or **Diwali** ticket. **Scan it** (or paste
the whole code — do not type just the short part).

**Pass:** 🔴 **WRONG EVENT**, and it **names the event the ticket is actually
for**, so you can point the guest at the right door.

### 7b. A refunded ticket

Scan the refunded practice ticket.

**Pass:** 🔴 **REFUNDED**, *"This ticket is not valid for entry"*, and **no way
to take money from them at any price.**

Then **search for them by name**. Check the search result row **also** says
*refunded* — and does **not** say "owes $0.00".

> This one had a real bug: the door once offered "Take cash $0.00 & admit" for
> somebody who already had their money back. Three different places on screen
> had to be fixed. Check all three.

### 7c. Nonsense code

Type `ZZZZZZZZZ`. **Pass:** 🔴 **NO MATCH**, and you can immediately try again.

### 7d. Two doors, one number — *expect this to fail*

Door A and Door B both look at the headcount on their own screens.
Now **both admit different guests** for two minutes.

**Expected:** the two phones show **different numbers**. The screen only updates
when *you* admit someone; it does not watch what the other door is doing.

**This is known.** We want to see how far apart they drift in two minutes, and
whether that would mislead anyone on the night. Coordinator: write the two
numbers down.

### 7e. No network

Door B turns on **airplane mode** and tries to admit somebody.

**Pass:** it fails **visibly** — 🔴 and *"Try again — nothing was recorded"*.
Nothing should half-happen.

Turn WiFi back on, scan the same guest.

**Pass:** they admit normally, exactly once.

> 📴 There is no offline mode, and there cannot be one. **Coordinator: decide
> now, out loud, who makes the "network is down, take cash and write names on
> paper" call, and make sure there is a printed guest list in the box.**

## Round 8 — Does the money add up? (15 min)

Coordinator, on `/dashboard`:

1. Read **Collected**, and the split by payment method
2. Tap **Export reconciliation CSV**
3. Compare three numbers:

| | Where |
|---|---|
| Cash taken | count the actual cash box |
| Cash on screen | dashboard, CASH row |
| People admitted | the door headcount |

**Pass:** the cash box matches the CASH row exactly.

**Expect a mismatch on heads** — comped guests are counted at the door but do not
appear in the money at all. Note the size of the gap.

## Things that are already known — please don't report these

We know. They are on the list.

1. **No "amount tendered" or change due** on cash screens
2. **No undo** — a wrongly admitted guest cannot be un-admitted
3. **Sticks are all-or-nothing** — you cannot hand over 2 of 4
4. **Comped guests leave no money trail**
5. **Walk-up buyers get no email** — their code is on screen only
6. **The two doors show different headcounts** (Round 7d)
7. **Dandiya is no-refunds**, so every screen says *"All sales are final"*

## Found something? Write it down

One line each. Paper is fine.

```
Who        Round   I tapped…            The screen said…        I expected…
Door B     2b      scanned Bala twice   green ADMITTED again    amber ALREADY IN
```

The three that matter most:

- Anything **green that should have been amber or red** — someone gets in free
- Anything you **had to read twice** — that is two seconds per guest, times 400
- Anywhere you **didn't know what to do next**

## Coordinator: setup and reset

<details>
<summary>Technical — before and after the rehearsal</summary>

**The practice event's scan window is six hours long.** Re-run the setup on the
morning of the rehearsal or the station will say *"Nothing to scan right now"*:

```bash
ENV_FILE=<composed env> npx tsx scripts/setup-rehearsal.ts --commit
```

Add `--send` to mint and email the roster tickets. Without it, nothing is sent.
Re-running is safe — already-confirmed orders are skipped.

**Pre-flight, the day before:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Test-login is on | `TEST_LOGIN_ENABLED=true`, password set and not the published default |
| Stripe is in **test** mode | otherwise rehearsal sales are real money |
| The Stripe webhook points at `test.dcica.org` | otherwise purchases never confirm |
| `/admin/email` sends | the tickets depend on it |
| **Freeze the `test` branch** | a push mid-rehearsal re-runs the seeds and rewrites fixtures |
| Wake the database — load `/api/health` | it sleeps after a week idle |

**Reset between runs:** re-run the setup script. It re-times the event, restores
capacity, and skips anyone already ticketed.

**Never** run `npm run verify` against the test database — several of its suites
delete events and users.

</details>
